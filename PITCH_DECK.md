## SLIDE 5 — PRODUCT SHOWCASE

### What Skillora Looks Like in Action

#### A. SkillUp (Youth Learners)
- **Self-paced courses** — 4–8 weeks, mobile-first
- **6 vocational tracks** aligned to Nigeria's economic priorities:
  - 💻 Digital Marketing Mastery
  - 👗 Fashion Design & Business
  - ☀️ Solar Installation Technology
  - 🌾 Agribusiness Fundamentals
  - 💰 Financial Literacy (Nigeria context)
  - 💻 Coding Basics & Web Dev
- **Shareable certificates** with QR-code verification
- **Gamified progress** — streaks, badges, leaderboards

#### B. EduPro (Teacher CPD)
- **4 CPD modules** mapped to TRCN renewal requirements:
  - Digital Classroom Skills (3 weeks)
  - Vocational Teaching Methods (4 weeks)
  - Inclusive Education (3 weeks)
  - Entrepreneurship Education (4 weeks)
- **AI Lesson Planner** generates:
  - Full lesson notes (definition, explanation, worked examples)
  - Daily life applications of the topic
  - 5-question class work with model answers
  - Downloadable as DOCX or PDF
- **CPD Certificates** with TRCN ID, school, and state — print-ready
